# 🚀 Digital Transformation Lab

### Exploring No-Code, Low-Code, RPA & AI-Assisted Product Development

> A hands-on portfolio exploring how emerging digital tools can transform business processes, automate workflows, build applications, and accelerate product experimentation.

---

## 📌 About This Repository

This repository documents my hands-on exploration of **no-code, low-code, robotic process automation (RPA), ERP platforms, and AI-assisted development tools** as part of my learning in **Building Digital Transformation**.

Rather than looking at these platforms only as technologies, I explore how they can be applied to **real-world business problems** — from workflow automation and repetitive task elimination to application development and integrated enterprise processes.

The objective is to understand:

**Business Problem → Process → Technology → Solution → Business Impact**

## 🔍 What I Explore

For each platform, I focus on five key questions:

### 01 — What business problem does it solve?
Understanding the operational problem and identifying where technology can create value.

### 02 — How does the process work?
Mapping the existing workflow and identifying bottlenecks, manual interventions, and opportunities for redesign.

### 03 — How can the technology solve it?
Designing a digital workflow, application, automation, or integrated system.

### 04 — What value can it create?
Evaluating improvements in areas such as:

- Process efficiency
- Automation
- Visibility
- User experience
- Scalability
- Cost
- Decision-making

### 05 — What did I learn?
Documenting the practical trade-offs, limitations, and situations where the technology is most appropriate.
